# Sunset Arena Rally / 선셋 아레나 랠리 — `tracks/spark_circuit/sunset_arena_rally.ctd`
D3, 2 × 1950 m, w 13–15. Signature `jump, surfaces, stacked`: all built, no fallbacks.
- Topology: a compact curl. The gravel rally stage runs north (rally jump 1, the wooded dirt S), then curls 270° left
  (off-camber, ridge) and crosses back over its own stage on a 9 m bridge, where jump 1 lands right under it. The
  single z-separated crossing gives turning number 2, which V1/V2 allow.
- Outer loop: quarry hook, or the `service_road` dirt shortcut (aiMin 0.45) that skips it; the tarmac return; the forest
  S; rally jump 2 on the north leg; the paddock; the grandstand S and sweep; then the stadium tunnel into the bowl.
- The bowl hairpin uses a custom banked profile (`PROFILE bowl`). The stadium tunnel and bridge girders are PROP
  landmarks.
- Legend ghost 54.1 s (table 55.7 s, −2.8%). Bench: speed/pro 8/8, item/racer 8/8, 0 respawns, ≤ 0.06 hard hits per
  bot-lap.
- History: jump 2 was moved off the tarmac return (it caused respawn loops 17 m after the branch merge). A 20 m run-off
  was added before the off-camber corner for the merged physics.
