## English
Glossy vinyl-toy chibi mascot portrait, head-and-shoulders, three-quarter view facing slightly left. The character "Pixel" is a true-voxel Clawd built from real visible cubes with flat 8-bit shading steps and grey #8B8B8B pixel trim. It is a rounded voxel-block Clawd creature (proportions 1.0 wide, 0.68 tall, 0.64 deep, soft rounded edges) with two vertical eye slots (unless the costume replaces them), no mouth, small stub arms, and a small original ten-ray sparkle floating just above its head. Clearcoat vinyl material, warm peach rim light, soft studio lighting on a transparent background, crisp silhouette, expression shown only through the eyes. Centered, fills 80% of the frame. No text.
