## English
Wide cinematic loading screen for the track "Snowglobe Halfpipe": a glittering ice halfpipe, a penguin village of snow cabins and a crystal cave. Three Clawd kart racers drift through the scene in a tight pack, drift sparks and boost flames glowing. Theme palette #BEE9F7, #F7FBFF, #2F6FA6, #6CF2C2, #B57CFF, long soft shadows. Dutch angle, wide lens, layered foreground, midground (racers) and background (landmarks). Leave the lower-left quarter calm for the title overlay. No text.
