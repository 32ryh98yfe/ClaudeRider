## English
Wide cinematic loading screen for the track "Orbital Express": sky rails around a space station, a vertical loop and a deep starfield with a ringed planet. Three Clawd kart racers drift through the scene in a tight pack, drift sparks and boost flames glowing. Theme palette #EEF3F8, #7DE2FC, #D97757, #0B1026, #A6FFCB, long soft shadows. Dutch angle, wide lens, layered foreground, midground (racers) and background (landmarks). Leave the lower-left quarter calm for the title overlay. No text.
