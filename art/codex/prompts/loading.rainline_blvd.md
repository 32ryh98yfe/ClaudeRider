## English
Wide cinematic loading screen for the track "Rainline Boulevard": a rainy neon city boulevard with glowing signs, reflective wet asphalt, traffic and an overpass. Three Clawd kart racers drift through the scene in a tight pack, drift sparks and boost flames glowing. Theme palette #1C1F26, #FF3EA5, #3EE6FF, #FFB347, #6A4C93, long soft shadows. Dutch angle, wide lens, layered foreground, midground (racers) and background (landmarks). Leave the lower-left quarter calm for the title overlay. No text.
